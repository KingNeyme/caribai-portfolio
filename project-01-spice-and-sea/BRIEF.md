# Client Brief — Spice & Sea Restaurant

| | |
|---|---|
| **Client** | Spice & Sea Restaurant: Marcia Joseph, owner |
| **Location** | Castries, Saint Lucia |
| **Business** | 60-seat restaurant, lunch and dinner; locals, tourists and cruise visitors |
| **Engagement** | Operational analytics: diagnosis, recommendations, and an ongoing dashboard |
| **Data period** | January 2024 – December 2025 |

---

## The request

> *"We're busier than ever but at the end of the month there's less money left.
> I don't know if it's food costs, staff, the menu, or the slow season.
> I need to know what's actually going on and what I should change."*

## Background

- The restaurant upgraded its point-of-sale (POS) system in **January 2025**, so sales
  history comes from two different systems.
- The head chef keeps a **monthly costing sheet** with the cost of each plate.
- Food is supplied by four suppliers, one of which **invoices in US dollars**.
- Staff clock in and out on a tablet.
- The owner believes **cruise ship days** bring in more customers.

## Business questions

1. **Growth:** is revenue actually growing, month over month and year over year?
2. **Food cost:** what is happening to food cost as a share of revenue, and which items drive it?
3. **Labour:** does labour cost track demand, or is the restaurant overstaffed at times?
4. **Menu profitability:** which items earn the most *profit*, not just the most sales?
5. **Seasonality:** how large are the swings around Carnival, Jounen Kwéyòl, cruise season,
   Christmas and the August–September slow period?

## Deliverables

| Deliverable | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Data inventory & issues log | Document what the data contains and every quality problem found |
| Cleaned dataset | A trustworthy single source, with every cleaning decision recorded |
| Analysis notebook | Reproducible answers to each business question |
| Owner memo (one page) | What's happening, how confident we are, and what to change |
| Operations dashboard | Weekly view of revenue, food cost %, labour %, top items and seasonality |

## Success criteria

- The owner can name the **main drivers** of the margin decline, backed by numbers.
- Each recommendation is **specific and actionable** (e.g. a price, a supplier, a roster change).
- The dashboard lets the owner track these numbers **without needing an analyst**.
